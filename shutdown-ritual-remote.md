## The Complete Shutdown Ritual Framework

1. **Map Your Priorities**  
   Review the day’s agenda and identify your top 1-2 critical items.

2. **Schedule Wrap-Up Time**  
   Block the final 30-45 minutes in your calendar specifically for shutdown activities.

3. **Screen Transition Ritual**  
   Close all work-related tabs and applications; switch to a personal background image or focus on a non-team strip.

4. **Digital Disconnection**  
   Turn off work-related notifications, disable Slack mobile alerts, and disengage from email accounts.

5. **Physical Action Ritual**  
   Perform a consistent physical action: stand up, stretch, switch into a different room, or put on “home mode” attire.

6. **Journaling Trigger**  
   Write a brief reflection: “What went well today?” and “What needs follow-up tomorrow?”

7. **Positive Closure Statement**  
   Conclude with a personal affirmation: “Work ended. I’m transitioning fully to personal time.”
